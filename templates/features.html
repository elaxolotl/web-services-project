What Gavel offers

Streamlined Auction Management with Automated Winner Assignment

Gavel API includes a daily task that automatically assigns auction winners, ensuring fairness and efficiency. The task checks for auctions that have reached their due date and automatically assigns a winner by selecting the highest bid.

Priority Handling of Perishable Goods

Our system prioritizes perishable goods during searches, ensuring that items closest to their expiration date are processed first. This feature helps prevent spoilage and ensures timely processing.
